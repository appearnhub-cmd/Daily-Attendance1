DAILY STAFF ATTENDANCE - PWA

Hosting (free, 2 minute):
1. Go to https://app.netlify.com/drop
2. Unzip this file, then drag the whole "attendance-pwa" folder onto the page.
3. You get a link (https). Open it on your phone.
4. Chrome: menu > "Install app".  iPhone Safari: Share > "Add to Home Screen".

GitHub Pages also works: upload all files to a repo and enable Pages.

Note: PWA works only over https (or localhost), not by opening index.html directly.
To update the app later, change the CACHE name in sw.js (attendance-v1 -> attendance-v2) and upload again.
